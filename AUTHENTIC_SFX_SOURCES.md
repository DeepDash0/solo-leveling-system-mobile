## Project policy
1. V6 ships **zero** generated, royalty-free substitute, or claimed-anime SFX files.
2. Every in-app event remains silent until a user manually supplies its specific audio file.
3. Imports are byte-preserving and browser-local (IndexedDB); no remote upload, no automatic hotlinking, no pitch shifting or sample synthesis.
4. Audio imported on the PC2 Tailscale origin does not magically propagate to public GitHub Pages, nor to another phone/browser.
5. The app cannot independently authenticate the source or legal rights of user-provided clips. Public deployment of audio assets requires appropriate redistribution permission.
6. Do not add recorded copyrighted media to `sounds/` or the public repo without appropriate rights.
